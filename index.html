Biology glossary

ATPA molecule cells use to transfer energy.
CellThe smallest unit generally considered living.
DNAThe molecule that stores hereditary information.
EnzymeA biological catalyst that speeds up reactions.
GeneA DNA sequence contributing to a functional product.
HomeostasisRegulation that helps maintain stable internal conditions.
MutationA change in a DNA sequence.
OrganelleA specialized structure inside a cell.
SpeciesA group defined by a scientific species concept.
MicroorganismAn organism too small to see clearly without magnification.
HabitatThe place where an organism lives.
AdaptationAn inherited feature that can improve reproductive success in an environment.
A useful study route

Where should you start?

A good order for learning biology is cells → molecules → genetics → organisms → evolution → ecology. Each level builds on ideas from the previous one.

🧪

Think like a biologist

Ask a question, make a testable prediction, collect evidence, and revise your explanation when the evidence requires it. Scientific models are useful tools, not perfect copies of nature.

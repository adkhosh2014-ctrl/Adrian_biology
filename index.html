Tiny details, huge impact

Did you know?

~3 billion

DNA letters in a human genome

The human genome contains roughly three billion base pairs of DNA. Those chemical letters help encode the instructions used to build and maintain a human body.

🔬

Biology connects many sciences

To understand living things, biologists use chemistry to study molecules, physics to understand movement and energy, and mathematics to interpret patterns in nature.
